Wishbar is a Mac menu bar app for live widgets. The product page says you type what you want in plain English and your own AI builds a real, working widget, one click away in your menu bar. The AI is the user's own ChatGPT (through Codex) or Claude. It is paid, pay once, with a free 7-day trial, and needs macOS 13 or later.
