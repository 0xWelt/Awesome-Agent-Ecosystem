Free browser editor for App Store and Google Play screenshots. All slides sit on one wide canvas with iPhone and Android phone frames, captions and gradient backgrounds, and each slide exports as a PNG at store size. Agents can drive the editor through its hosted MCP server at `https://appscreen.pro/mcp`: lay out slides, drop app screenshots into the frames, style backgrounds and export. Setup guide: https://appscreen.pro/mcp
