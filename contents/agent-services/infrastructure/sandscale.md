Firecracker microVM sandboxes, workspaces, build hosts and lab hosts for AI coding agents, one microVM per run, with allow/ask/deny policy per tool, human approvals, DLP, a secrets vault and an audit log. Free plan.

The vendor publishes a remote MCP server (Streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1) at `https://mcp.sandscale.dev/mcp` and the `sandscale` CLI.
